How I work

Principles that survive contact with production.

The same three convictions, whether the stack is twenty years old or twenty days old.

01

Ship boring, disciplined increments.

Small, reversible changes beat big-bang rewrites. Boring is a feature when money is on the line.

02

Observability before features.

If you can't see it, you can't defend it. Instrumentation comes first, then the thing you wanted to build.

03

Skeptical of trends, not of progress.

Adopt what earns its keep. New tools get the same scrutiny as old ones — and AI is no exception.
