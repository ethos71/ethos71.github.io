## 1. Data Cake

**Domain**: Synthetic training-data for a regulated industry  
**Status**: Patent Pending

Tax AI can't be trained on real customer transactions — the data is PII-laden, regulated, and
contractually restricted. Data Cake addresses the training-data bottleneck so ML projects in tax
can move forward without putting customer data at risk.

**Why it matters**: New ML projects stopped being a regulatory negotiation. Training-data setup
went from a quarter-long compliance review to days — and customer data stayed where it belonged.

## 2. Audit Management Timber Model

**Domain**: Tax audit-defense decision support  
**Status**: Patent Pending

Fortune 500 tax teams routinely manage dozens or hundreds of open audit findings, each one a
contest/settle/absorb decision with real legal and financial weight. The Timber Model turns that
portfolio into something teams can reason about systematically.

**Why it matters**: $50K judgment calls that used to be gut-feel or spreadsheet-driven became
data-driven and explainable, with reasoning trails an auditor can follow.

## 3. Doc Intel

**Domain**: Developer tooling for regulated environments  
**Status**: Patent Pending

Doc Intel surfaces the gap between source code and the documentation that's supposed to describe
it — making documentation drift a visible, measurable signal instead of a silent liability.

**Why it matters**: In regulated environments — audit trails, SOX-relevant docs, tax-treatment
justifications — drift between code and documentation isn't an annoyance. It's an audit finding
waiting to happen. Doc Intel turns that into something teams can catch in advance.

## 4. Mort AI Nexus

**Domain**: Multi-State Economic Nexus Compliance (Sales Tax)  
**Status**: Patent Pending

A predictive system that forecasts when a company is on track to cross **economic nexus
thresholds** across US jurisdictions in the post-*Wayfair* sales-tax landscape. Each state sets
its own threshold (typically $100K in revenue or 200 transactions per year — see the [Sales Tax
Institute's economic nexus state
guide](https://www.salestaxinstitute.com/resources/economic-nexus-state-guide)). Cross the line
and you owe sales tax in that state, often discovered months later via a state notice with
penalties and interest stacked on top.

**Why it matters**: Per-state exposure runs $100K–$500K. Mort AI Nexus shifts discovery from
reactive ("we got a state notice") to predictive ("we're on track to cross — here's what to do").

[Read the deep dive →](/engineering/ai/2026/01/14/mort-ai-nexus-patent.html)
